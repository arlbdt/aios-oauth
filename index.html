AIOS

AIOS is a personal tool with a single user: its developer. It connects to the developer's own Google account to read and organise the developer's own Gmail and to file the developer's own documents in Google Drive. It is not offered to the public.

Permissions

  • Read Gmail (gmail.readonly): to index and search the developer's own email on the developer's computer.
  • Organise Gmail (gmail.modify, gmail.settings.basic): to apply and remove labels, archive messages, create filters, and move newsletters from senders the developer has unsubscribed from to the Trash. Each new rule is approved by the developer first.
  • Create drafts (gmail.compose): to prepare email drafts that the developer reviews and sends.
  • Google Drive (drive): to file the developer's own documents in the developer's own folders.

AIOS never sends email, never permanently deletes email, and never forwards email.

Privacy policy

  • Messages and documents are stored and indexed on the developer's own computer and in the developer's own Google account, and processed by AI assistants working for the developer. They are not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.
  • AIOS collects no data about anyone else.
  • AIOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
