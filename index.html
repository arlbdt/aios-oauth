AIOS

AIOS is a personal tool with a single user: its developer. It connects to the developer's own Google account to read the developer's own Gmail messages. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

  • AIOS asks Google for one permission: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Messages are stored and indexed on the developer's own computer and processed by AI assistants working for the developer. They are not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used for advertising.
  • AIOS collects no data about anyone else.
  • AIOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms of service

AIOS is provided for the developer's own use only, without warranty.
